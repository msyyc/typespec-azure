# Documentation quality migration

## 1. Conclusion and scope

The TypeSpec linter `@azure-tools/typespec-azure-core/documentation-required` combines [DescriptionMustNotBeNodeName](https://github.com/Azure/azure-openapi-validator/blob/main/docs/description-must-not-be-node-name.md) and [DescriptiveDescriptionRequired](https://github.com/Azure/azure-openapi-validator/blob/main/docs/descriptive-description-required.md) with its existing missing-documentation requirements. **It is not functionally equivalent over arbitrary emitted Swagger graphs.** Authored names, compiler-exposed documentation, and source declarations define its policy; generated names and repeated emitted references do not. It intentionally rejects empty descriptions missed by the Swagger linter's truthy-selector false-negative bug. Current-head corpus coverage remains unassessed. Completed CI compatibility findings have been independently reconciled into a suppression-only consumer proposal; that proposal and the unknown tested consumer SHA do not establish green integration or merge readiness.

## 2. Actual coverage

The two member investigations each attempted 468 ARM projects at the same frozen specs revision, with 462 successful compilations and six exclusions. They used separate TypeSpec linter runs and different source/closure revisions. These are historical observations, not a run of the consolidated official rule. Reported diagnostics are individual findings, not project counts.

| Count                                                                         | Swagger linter | TypeSpec linter |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | --------------- |
| DescriptionMustNotBeNodeName: historical successful-project raw diagnostics   | 70,815         | 7,523           |
| DescriptiveDescriptionRequired: historical successful-project raw diagnostics | 0              | 5               |
| Consolidated official rule at the inspected head: corpus diagnostics          | Unassessed     | Unassessed      |

**Assessed coverage:** the name-check investigation observed all 105 Swagger-affected projects among 143 TypeSpec-affected projects; selected-version attribution retained 7,501 findings in 142 projects. This establishes project overlap for that historical implementation, not matched violations or a coverage percentage. The blank-check investigation explained all five findings in three successful projects: four emitted empty strings skipped by the Swagger selector and one empty inherited-property description omitted by the emitter. Those observations support the documented predicate and population differences. They do not measure the current authored-name adaptation or the combined rule's additional missing-documentation findings. Comparable combined violation totals and coverage percentages are **unassessed**; current-source corpus results and a collision-resistant cross-engine correspondence are unavailable.

See [Why diagnostic counts differ](#4-why-diagnostic-counts-differ) and the [name-check](members/DescriptionMustNotBeNodeName/migration.md) and [blank-check](members/DescriptiveDescriptionRequired/migration.md) reports. Do not sum the two historical TypeSpec runs or attribute the combined rule's `default` diagnostics to either quality check.
